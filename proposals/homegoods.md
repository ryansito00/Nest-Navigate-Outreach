# Nest Navigate x HomeGoods
### Rewards Partnership Proposal

---

## Who We Are

Nest Navigate is a platform that guides first-time homebuyers through the homebuying journey using milestone-based education and rewards. As users complete key steps — getting pre-approved, understanding closing costs, making their first offer — they earn rewards redeemable at select local and national partners.

---

## Why HomeGoods

There's no better moment to introduce a new homeowner to HomeGoods than when they're furnishing their very first place. First-time buyers are starting from scratch — new space, blank walls, empty rooms. A HomeGoods reward delivered right around closing gives buyers a reason to walk through your doors at the most exciting moment of their lives.

---

## How It Works

| Step | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **We pre-buy gift cards** | Nest Navigate purchases $250 in HomeGoods gift cards upfront — no cost, no risk to you |
| **Users earn rewards** | Buyers redeem a gift card upon completing a key homebuying milestone on our platform |
| **You get visibility** | HomeGoods is featured on our partner page and highlighted in milestone completion emails |
| **We grow together** | As our user base scales, so does the volume of gift cards we purchase |

---

## The Gift Card Strategy

We don't ask partners to sponsor or donate anything. We pre-buy gift cards at face value — starting at **$250 per location** — and use them as earned rewards on our platform. This means:

- **Zero cost to join.** We're a paying customer from day one.
- **Guaranteed redemption.** Gift cards are earned, not randomly distributed, so users are motivated and ready to spend.
- **Immediate activation.** We feature your brand on the site as soon as the first purchase is made.

---

## Our Users

- First-time homebuyers in the homebuying process (pre-approval through closing)
- Motivated, emotionally invested, and actively preparing to furnish and decorate
- Primed to establish new shopping habits with home décor brands at exactly the right life stage
